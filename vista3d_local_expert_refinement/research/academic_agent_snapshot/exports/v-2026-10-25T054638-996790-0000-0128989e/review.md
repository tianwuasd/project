## 研究问题

- Existing multimodal agents often select visual experts using textual outputs or heuristics and lack quantitative criteria for deciding when visual assistance is needed or which expert to invoke\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- MLLMs struggle with fine-grained pixel understanding, while VQA and visual grounding do not effectively assess this capability\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- The study addresses learning to defer to multiple experts when a predictor is already available and jointly retraining it with a deferral function could be prohibitively costly, potentially taking weeks or months\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- The paper addresses scale imbalance in multi-target semi-supervised medical image segmentation: large targets may receive priority, causing smaller targets and background to be misclassified as larger structures\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- The paper addresses interactive segmentation errors around fine details, small objects and complex boundaries, and refinement loops that distribute effort uniformly rather than targeting critical error regions\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- Sparse, filamentous neurons and substantial noise make 3D fluorescence microscopy segmentation difficult; limited receptive fields in sliding-window inference hinder long-range morphology modeling and can produce fragmented masks\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- Training reinforcement-learning policies over many environment variations can be inefficient and plateau at suboptimal performance\. [ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d](#ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d)
- Manual 3D medical image segmentation is time-consuming, while task-specific expert models require data curation and engineering expertise; rare pathologies and animal data also motivate zero-shot segmentation and interactive correction\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- Medical image segmentation can degrade when one or more modalities contain imperfections; existing models may fail to detect these imperfections or dynamically adjust to data quality, particularly when test and training data differ substantially\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- Whole heart segmentation in congenital heart disease is challenged by extreme anatomical variability, limited labeled data, low cardiac MR signal-to-noise ratio and resolution, weak boundaries between structures, and stent artifacts\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Most existing learning-to-defer methods make instance-level routing decisions, whereas segmentation requires spatially varying decisions near ambiguous boundaries, low-contrast tissues and small lesions\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- Rare CHD types make large training cohorts difficult to acquire, while prior cardiac generative approaches primarily model normal topology and do not readily capture CHD topological variations\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)

## 方法

- DnR reformulates a question into concrete visual queries, grounds each query with a CLIP-based model, and averages the resulting spatial relevance maps\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- The utilization metric combines semantic change after masking relevant regions with prediction stability after masking irrelevant regions; its adaptive weighting uses relevance-map entropy and contrast\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- Expert boxes, masks, or depth maps are rendered onto the original image for re-querying through the existing vision encoder without architectural modification\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- DnR recomputes utilization for expert-rendered candidates using the original masks, selects the largest positive improvement over the draft, and retains the draft if no expert improves utilization\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- HLMAT models interactive segmentation as a multi-step Markov Decision Process in which MLLMs generate text-based coordinates without specialized architectures or implicit tokens\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- SegAgent receives the current mask overlaid on the image and a task and object description, then outputs a textual click action; an interactive segmentation model uses the action history, current mask and image to update the mask\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- Training trajectories are simulated from ground-truth masks by placing clicks in false-positive or false-negative regions; generation limits trajectory length, stops at a reward threshold and discards actions below a reward-improvement threshold\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- StaR\+ retains rollout actions that increase reward, replaces reward-decreasing actions with simulator corrections, and fine-tunes on the corrected trajectories merged with the original trajectory dataset\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- At inference, the MLLM predicts the current mask's mIoU as a process reward\. Heuristic greedy search samples K candidate actions, evaluates their resulting masks using PRM, selects the highest predicted reward and returns the mask with the highest predicted reward across steps\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- Evaluation uses cumulative IoU, with maximum steps T=7 and K=1 for RES, and T=11 and K=3 for HRES\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- The approach first trains a predictor with a standard classification surrogate such as logistic loss, then learns a deferral function while keeping the predictor's scoring function fixed\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- In the score-based setting, the second-stage surrogate combines a classification surrogate weighted by the indicator that the predictor is correct with expert-specific surrogate terms weighted by 1 minus each expert's deferral cost\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- Training used logistic losses, Adam, batch size 128 and weight decay 1×10−4, for 15 epochs on SVHN and 50 epochs on CIFAR-10, without data augmentation\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- CGS uses a teacher–student framework with weak-to-strong consistency, a general segmentation branch, and class-specific specialist heads that distinguish background, their target class, and all remaining target classes\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- The teacher is updated by exponential moving average of the student; supervised and unsupervised segmentation losses combine cross-entropy and Dice losses\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- Bidirectional cross-branch consistency exchanges pseudo-labels between the generalist and specialists, while a consensus-based loss jointly optimizes regions where the branches agree\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- IHED filters specialist pseudo-labels using inter-head agreement: either every head predicts background, or one head predicts its target and all other heads predict their remaining classes\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- The architecture uses a shared MAE-pretrained plain ViT with an FPN; positive and negative click disk maps are concatenated with the previous segmentation mask and embedded alongside image patches\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- An auxiliary edge head receives supervision from edge maps generated from segmentation masks using differential operators such as Laplacian or Sobel filters; training combines normalized focal segmentation loss with Dice edge loss\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- U-CFR computes predictive uncertainty as 1−\|2Pseg−1\| and multiplies it by the normalized Sobel gradient magnitude of the segmentation probability map to obtain boundary-aware uncertainty\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- After each user interaction, U-CFR selects the maximum boundary-aware uncertainty location as a pseudo-click candidate\. It skips candidates with probabilities in the inclusive interval \[0\.49, 0\.51\], assigns positive labels above 0\.51 and negative labels below 0\.49, then feeds accepted clicks back into the model\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- NeuroRefiner iteratively combines global inspection, region-specific instruction generation, TopoRefineNet editing, and validation of local refinements; it stops when morphology is judged complete or the maximum iteration count is reached\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- The Global Inspector uses global and patch-level mask projections together with the zeroth Betti number, computed as the connected-component count in each corresponding 3D region, to identify blocks with false-positive or false-negative errors\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- The Refinement Advisor compares image and mask projections in the xy and yz planes and generates instructions specifying both a spatial location and an editing operation\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- The main method description requires the Change Validator to check instruction adherence and topological improvement, retrying failed edits with new instructions and escalating persistently unresolved blocks to human experts\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- TopoRefineNet encodes the concatenated image and noisy mask, embeds instructions with a frozen pretrained text encoder, and fuses text with the deepest visual features using cross-attention before decoding a refined mask\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- TopoRefineNet uses two training stages: synthetic morphological defects with perturbation-derived instructions, followed by real segmentation errors with instructions synthesized using Qwen3-VL\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- Experiments use Qwen3-VL-8B for all agents, Qwen3 Embedding 0\.6B for text embeddings, 128×128-pixel inspection regions, and a maximum of 5 iterations\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- GSL trains a generalist until a performance plateau, initializes specialists from its checkpoint on disjoint subsets of the lowest-performing variations, and resumes generalist training using demonstrations from specialists and from the generalist on remaining variations\. [ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d](#ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d)
- The experiments combine PPO or PPG with DAPG on Procgen, PPO with DAPG on Meta-World, and SAC with GAIL on ManiSkill, including online environment interactions during demonstration-guided learning\. [ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d](#ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d)
- The model uses a MONAI SegResNet CNN backbone, 128-voxel cubic training patches, and sliding-window inference\. Its automatic head accepts a class index and produces a binary segmentation; its interactive head accepts positive and negative 3D clicks\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- Interactive refinement merges the independent branch outputs by adding or removing only connected-component regions containing the relevant clicks, aiming to avoid unintended changes to correct segmentation regions\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- The proposed supervoxel method sums upsampled SAM ViT-H features extracted in axial, coronal, and sagittal views, then applies SLIC with nsegments = 100 and sigma = 3\. These supervoxels train the interactive branch to transfer SAM knowledge to 3D segmentation\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- Training proceeds through interactive-branch training, interactive finetuning with dataset oversampling, automatic-branch training with the encoder frozen, and automatic finetuning using MAISI-generated anomalies to augment under-represented classes\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- REDNet processes 3D volumes with separate modality-specific subjective logic networks, evaluates intra-modality consistency and cross-modality differences, discounts evidence at each pixel, and fuses it using Dempster–Shafer evidence theory\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- ICEM compares evidence singular values with stored training-data singular values using the minimum class-normalized L1 distance, and combines this consistency metric with convolutional features\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- CDAM constructs differential evidence between modalities and processes concatenated evidence through convolutional layers; larger discrepancies reduce the cross-modality weight and lead to greater discounting\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- The discounting module combines intra-modality and cross-modality information, with both weighting hyperparameters set to 1, before merging discounted opinions using Reduced Dempster’s Combination Rule\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- Training used a learning rate of 1e-4 for 200 epochs, initial single-modality training followed by multi-modality training, and 128 × 128 × 128 crops\. Evaluation used sliding windows with 0\.6 overlap, Dice and HD95\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- The task labels LV, RV, LA including pulmonary veins, RA, AO, PA, SVC and IVC from a single-phase 3D cardiac MR scan and one click per structure; myocardium segmentation and time-series imaging are outside the stated focus\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- The recurrent model uses a modified 3D U-Net to jointly predict the next segmentation and a stopping indicator, feeding the predicted segmentation into the next iteration\. Users can override automatic stopping by selecting an earlier segmentation or requesting more iterations\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- A teacher-forcing sequence likelihood loss decomposes into segmentation and stopping losses over decoupled time steps, eliminating backpropagation through time\. Consecutive partial-segmentation training pairs are generated on the fly from complete ground truth\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Training targets prescribe constant-rate growth along vessel centerlines and outward chamber growth using concentric spheres intersected with ground truth\. Input segmentations are corrupted with nonrigid deformations and foreground blobs while output targets remain unchanged\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Experiments trained on 20 images and tested on 40, or trained on 48 images and tested on 12\. Training used four-fold cross-validation, and the four resulting networks were ensembled for test predictions\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- User interaction was simulated\. Iter–U selected the best segmentation among the first 40 iterations, while Iter–U–All selected the iteration with the best mean accuracy across all eight structures\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Dice evaluation excluded optional vessel regions from both predicted and ground truth segmentations, comparing only required regions\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- DeferredSeg adds J\+1 pixel-wise routing channels for a base segmentor and J experts while retaining the original segmentation output channels\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- The Deferral Collaboration loss rewards correct expert predictions, suppresses incorrect expert routing, rewards the model branch when its thresholded prediction is correct, and supervises segmentation probabilities with binary cross-entropy\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- Training combines collaboration loss, spatial-coherence loss and a load-balancing penalty; the latter penalizes batch-average soft expert routing probabilities outside configurable lower and upper bounds\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- At inference, each pixel is assigned to the branch with the largest routing logit\. Model-routed pixels use the base prediction thresholded at 0\.5; expert-routed pixels use the selected expert's prediction\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- The auxiliary CNN aggregated deferral predictor supports spatial-coherence training; at inference it is used only for visualization and does not determine hard routing or final fusion\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- PatchCorr uses the same expert source as one-expert DeferredSeg, with its correction budget matched to DeferredSeg's one-expert deferral ratio on each dataset\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- SDF4CHD disentangles CHD type and patient-specific shape using diagnosis-conditioned implicit anatomical templates, a deformation module, and a correction module for additional patient-specific topological variations\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
- The shape module uses neural ordinary differential equations to learn invertible deformations, with a spatial latent code of 4×4×4×27\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
- In practice, training used occupancy values rather than signed distances; anatomical boundaries were represented by the 0\.5-level set\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)

## 数据

- The comprehensive evaluation covers 16 multimodal benchmarks across VQA, image captioning, visual reasoning, knowledge VQA, and comprehensive evaluation, using dataset subsets, GRAY rendering, and Hybrid masking\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- Hallucination evaluation uses HaloQuest, MMHal-Bench, VizWiz, and COCO Caption with highlight rendering; ChatGPT 4o-mini categorizes responses as Hallucination, Misperception, Grounded, or Correct\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- RES comprises refCOCO, refCOCO\+ and refCOCOg\. HRES derives from HQSeg-44K using DIS5K and ThinObject5K; DIS5K contains 5,470 images across 22 groups and 225 fine-grained categories\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- Experiments used CIFAR-10 and SVHN, with ResNet-4 as both predictor and deferral model and ResNet-10, ResNet-16 and ResNet-28 as experts\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- ACDC contains scans from 100 patients with three segmentation targets and uses a 7:1:2 training/validation/test split\. SegTHOR has four targets and 28/4/8 training/validation/test volumes\. Synapse contains 30 CT scans annotated with eight abdominal organs and uses 18 training and 12 test cases\. Input slices are resized to 224 × 224\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- Experiments use three different seeds on each of the three datasets and report means and standard deviations\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- Training uses SBD, reported as containing 8,498 training images and 2,820 test images\. Evaluation includes SBD, COCO\_MVal, Pascal VOC, DAVIS, Berkeley, GrabCut, BraTS, ssTEM and OAIZIB\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- Out-of-domain evaluation uses BraTS, ssTEM, OAIZIB and a curated irradiated-metallic SEM precipitate dataset; models trained solely on SBD are applied without fine-tuning\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- Evaluation uses BigNeuron, CWMBS, and ZBFWB with previously established training–testing splits\. BigNeuron contains expert-annotated neurons from multiple species and imaging platforms; CWMBS includes 83 strong-noise and 162 weak-signal volumes of 256×256×256 voxels at 0\.2×0\.2×1 µm resolution\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)

- The second training stage selects 5,310 error-containing volumes of 128×128×64 voxels from predictions by 3D U-Net, V-Net, UNETR, nnFormer, and SwinUNETR, using connected-component and F1 scores\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)

- The curated dataset contains 11,454 CT volumes from in-house and public sources\. Each source is randomly split into 64% training, 16% validation, and 20% test data; TotalSegmentator supplies 117-class pseudo-labels and SAM supplies supervoxels for every scan, with unreliable pseudo-labels removed by post-processing\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- Zero-shot evaluation uses four external datasets: 140 mouse micro-CT scans at 0\.2 mm resolution with four annotated organs, 210 C4KC-KITS kidney-tumor scans, 53 adrenocortical-carcinoma scans, and 105 hepatocellular-carcinoma scans\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)

- The Changhai Hospital dataset includes 139 patients with serous or mucinous cystic neoplasms, arterial, portal venous and delayed phases, and radiologist annotations of pancreas and tumor regions; the three phases were registered to the venous phase using Elastix\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)

Severity categories described anatomical malformations, not clinical prognosis\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Evaluation uses synthetic expert masks on PROMISE12, LiTS and AMOS22, and pre-existing clinician annotations as offline expert branches on Chaksu, QUBIQ and CURVAS; metrics are DSC, Jaccard and Sensitivity\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)

- The study used imageCHD CT data and diagnosis labels with segmentations of seven cardiac structures, manually processed under radiology expert supervision; the reported splits contained 67 training, 8 validation, and 34 testing patients\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)

## 局限

- Rendering styles and parameters require dataset- and model-specific tuning; utilization levels and changes vary across datasets, architectures, and inputs, and Hybrid masking remains a heuristic sensitive to masking ratio, rendering style, and expert setup\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- DnR cannot recover conceptual facts absent from the model; the paper characterizes its benefit as improving access to existing knowledge rather than augmenting knowledge\. [ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c](#ref_1e05e2b0eb6c452c)
- Failures mainly involve incorrect initial target localization or inaccurate refinement coordinates, particularly points falling outside object boundaries\. The authors consider fine-grained pixel localization the principal performance bottleneck\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- Nearly optimal simulated trajectories lack diversity, constraining model generalization and robustness; noisy ground-truth masks can also produce erroneous training actions, especially near trajectory endpoints\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- More complex search strategies involve a computational complexity–performance trade-off; exploration of BFS and MCTS is left for future work\. [ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25](#ref_aa6dce0ed7f14b25)
- The authors report being unaware of established baselines for their two-stage deferral setting\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- The deferral cost function is typically selected through cross-validation in practice; developing a principled selection method is left for future research\. [ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3](#ref_5d1c00a2b3764ef3)
- At ACDC's 5% labeled setting, CGS has worse 95HD than BCP: 2\.42±0\.08 versus 1\.90\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- Training memory increases with the number of classes: at batch size 24 with teacher and student networks, reported GPU memory rises from 7\.13 GB for 3 classes to 10\.62 GB for 8 classes\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- The authors state that IHED and cross-branch consistency depend on the specialized segmentation heads, preventing independent ablations of these modules without that foundation\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- More comprehensive simultaneous handling of occurrence and scale imbalance remains future work; adding resampling to CGS raises Synapse average DSC from 60\.20% to 64\.93%\. [ref_6572d29cec464414](#ref_6572d29cec464414)
- U-CFR does not improve every refinement metric: Table 2 reports lower mIoU at 10 clicks than CFR on Pascal VOC \(97\.80% versus 97\.94%\), COCO\_MVal \(94\.92% versus 95\.11%\) and SBD \(92\.72% versus 92\.85%\)\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- The edge head is intended as a training-time regularizer rather than a geometrically precise standalone boundary detector; the paper notes that topology-aware losses could better capture thin-boundary connectivity\. [ref_dc3db188901f4579](#ref_dc3db188901f4579)
- NeuroRefiner's 2D maximum-intensity projections lose depth information in dense regions, hindering precise 3D error localization; neuron overlap can also interfere with inspection of some complex volumes\. [ref_864fba4def554ce3](#ref_864fba4def554ce3)
- ManiSkill evaluation is restricted to a reduced PushChair setup with eight chairs because physical simulation and point-cloud rendering are expensive, with an observed speed of 30 environment steps per second\. [ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d](#ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d)
- The plateau criterion requires hyperparameter tuning: starting too early loses initial generalist efficiency, while starting too late wastes samples and could make specialist-guided fine-tuning harder\. [ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d](#ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d)
- The DnC RL comparison was skipped because the authors found its quadratic computational complexity in the number of specialists computationally intractable in their setup\. [ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d](#ref_b8b1ef49ee17445d)
- In the supported-class evaluation, a 'single point' means one click per sliding-window patch, and a click affects only the 128-voxel cubic patch containing it; correction clicks are simulated from false-positive or false-negative regions\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- TotalSegV2 comparisons are biased toward nnUNet and TotalSegmentator because the ground truth was generated by pretrained TotalSegmentator, and its training data may include the study's test split\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- Extending support to MRI and PET, improving zero-shot interaction, and validating and integrating the workflow with clinical partners are identified as future work\. [ref_669c3001a51d42b8](#ref_669c3001a51d42b8)
- For test images from uCT 960\+ and iCT 256 scanners that differed from the training distribution, segmentation quality decreased, although the reported results remained anatomically consistent\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- Always-on discounting reduces belief masses even for perfectly reliable inputs, slightly diminishing usable information from high-quality sources despite discount factors close to 1\. [ref_e8706d7715e44ae8](#ref_e8706d7715e44ae8)
- Failures included chamber growth through septal defects or valves and failure to grow through narrow or artifact-affected main pulmonary artery trunks\. PA segmentation remained difficult generally, and some other structures remained difficult for particular pathologies\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Multiclass iterative models generally performed worse than binary models and gained less from simulated user stopping\. A possible explanation is that a single multiclass iteration may not simultaneously provide the best segmentation of every structure; multiclass procedures were inherited from tuning for binary segmentation\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Actual-user accuracy and the hypothesis of faster, more intuitive interaction remain to be assessed in a user study; the reported experiments used simulated users\. [ref_035a8553e2314bc2](#ref_035a8553e2314bc2)
- Adding experts does not guarantee better performance: benefits depend on regional complementarity and the target task\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- Training still requires previously annotated expert data, and the real-expert evaluation is offline; broader prospective studies are needed to assess clinical generality and interaction efficiency\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- A proof-of-concept Streamlit interface demonstrates expert-region assignment, local correction and fusion, but usability, annotation time, interaction cost and clinical efficiency remain unevaluated in prospective clinician studies\. [ref_750f4a75835240a3](#ref_750f4a75835240a3)
- The method failed to reconstruct an apical VSD in a test case with two VSDs because its learned templates contained only subaortic VSDs; the correction network also missed the apical defect\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
- The shared Gaussian shape prior across CHD types is a simplifying assumption; patient-specific shape distributions may differ by type and are difficult to capture with few examples\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
- Higher-resolution latent shape codes improved reconstruction but produced non-physiological distortions during synthetic generation\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
- The predicted diffeomorphic mapping does not guarantee strictly positive mesh-element Jacobians, despite reducing surface-intersection artifacts\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
- SDF4CHD and the baselines struggled to represent smaller vessel branches; generative modeling of vessel-connection variations was not extensively explored\. [ref_a3a141cb15434c22](#ref_a3a141cb15434c22)
