KEYNOTE SPEECH

## “Development and Deployment of Multimodal AI Foundation Models for Real-World Clinical Workflows”

Abstract: Conventional deep learning methods have been extensively investigated throughout end-to-end clinical workflows to support medical imaging, disease screening, clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, and prognostic prediction. Nevertheless, such task-specific models require separate development and optimization for individual clinical scenarios, leading to inefficient model development. Multimodal AI foundation models have fundamentally shifted this research paradigm by enabling a single unified model to accommodate multiple heterogeneous clinical tasks, greatly expediting AI development and real-world clinical translation. This talk will review traditional deep learning applications in routine clinical practice and present our recent progress in foundation model-enabled medical imaging solutions, demonstrating their promising capability to enhance clinical workflows.

## Motivation

Medical image analysis empowered by AI has significantly reshaped medicine. However, real-world medical image analysis poses significant challenges for medical AI, including domain shifts due to diverse imaging protocols and medical centers, variability in pathological regions of interest (ROIs) such as lesions and scars, and limited generalization capabilities of existing deep learning models. This challenge aims to encourage the development of novel and effective methods that facilitate the translation of medical AI into real-world clinical practice.
